# Metal-AD: A Large-Scale Dataset for Industrial Metal Anomaly Detection

> 🚧 **Status: Coming Soon** > The full dataset (including precise pixel-level masks and train/test splits) will be made publicly available upon the acceptance and publication of our manuscript. Stay tuned!

## 📖 About
Metal-AD is a large-scale, high-resolution dataset specifically constructed to address the unique challenges in industrial metal anomaly detection. It features 21,837 images across 10 categories, introducing complex real-world challenges such as Multiple Defect Instances (MDI) and Multiple Defect Types (MDT) to rigorously evaluate anomaly localization models.

## ✨ Key Features
* **Massive Scale:** 21,837 high-resolution images encompassing 10 distinct industrial metal categories.
* **Complex Scenarios:** Uniquely includes samples with Multiple Defect Instances (MDI) and Multiple Defect Types (MDT) within single images, presenting a higher level of difficulty compared to MVTec-AD and VisA.
* **Precise Annotations:** Comprehensive pixel-level ground truth masks provided for all 48 distinct defect types.
